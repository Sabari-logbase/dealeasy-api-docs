<Note>
  **Upcoming.** Gift with product campaigns aren't returned by the API yet.
</Note>

A **gift with product** campaign gives the buyer a free gift when they buy a qualifying product. For example, "Buy any coffee machine, get a free pack of beans".

When this type is released, the product endpoints will return these campaigns alongside volume discounts and BOGO, with their own `type` value and structure, documented on this page. Integrations that [skip unknown types](/campaigns/overview#product-campaign-types) won't need any change until you're ready to support it.
